Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and personalising your experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, so we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting and infrastructure. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for any purpose other than delivering the service to us.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app.
